Turf merchant API

Ship anywhere Turf's shipping companies cover in Syria with one integration. Create a shipment, and Turf assigns a shipping company immediately — the one you choose, or the best one by price, speed, distance or reliability. Then follow every step: status texts in Arabic and English, the courier's live position on a map, signed webhooks, and an embeddable tracking widget.

Authentication

Create a secret key in your dashboard under Developers → API keys and send it in the Authorization header:

Authorization: Bearer turf_sk_…

Keys starting with turf_test_ are test keys: same API, practice shipments only, nothing is sent to a shipping company.

Secret keys can create and cancel shipments. Use them only from your server — never in browser or mobile app code. For pages your customers see, use the public tracking API or the widget, which need no key.

Create a shipment

POST /shipments

The shipment is allocated to a carrier in the same request. The response is 201 with the shipment, or 200 (header Idempotent-Replayed: true) if a shipment with the same reference already exists — so retrying after a timeout never creates duplicates.

  • trackingUrl — a branded tracking page you can send to your customer (SMS, WhatsApp, email).
  • labelUrl — a printable 100×150 mm label with a barcode. Stick it on the parcel before pickup.

Choosing a carrier

With "carrier": "auto", Turf considers every active carrier that covers both governorates, accepts the weight, offers cash on delivery when you need it, and accepts automatic orders. Each is measured on:

  • price — from the carrier's published rates;
  • ETA — the carrier's promised delivery time, blended with its real delivery times once it has history;
  • distance — from the carrier's nearest hub to the pickup point;
  • reliability — share of finished shipments that were delivered (not returned);
  • load — open shipments per courier right now.
strategyPicks
balancedThe best overall score (price 30%, ETA 25%, reliability 25%, distance 10%, load 10%).
cheapestThe lowest price.
fastestThe shortest ETA.
nearestThe closest hub to your pickup point.
reliableThe highest delivery success rate.

Carriers can serve only some cities of a governorate, or only addresses near their hubs; an address outside that is not offered to them. Send location with the address for the best matches.

More than one company on the way

Turf also looks for chains of up to three companies: company A carries the parcel to a hub of company B in a governorate in between, B carries it on, and the last company delivers it (for example Aleppo to Homs with A, Homs to Damascus with B). Chains compete with single companies on price, time and reliability, so a chain can win even when one company covers the whole route. You still get one shipment, one tracking number, one price and one tracking page. The shipment then has a route:

"route": [
  { "carrier": { "id": "…", "name": "North Express" }, "from": "HL", "to": "HI", "handoverHub": { "name": "Homs Central", "city": "Homs" }, "price": { "amount": 9000, "currency": "SYP" }, "status": "done" },
  { "carrier": { "id": "…", "name": "Sham Express" }, "from": "HI", "to": null, "handoverHub": null, "price": { "amount": 6000, "currency": "SYP" }, "status": "active" }
]

carrier on the shipment is always the company that has the parcel now. The handover appears in the events (handed_over), and cash on delivery is collected by the last company.

Ties are broken by the balanced score, then by fewer handovers. Routes that cost more than 2.5 times the cheapest one, or take more than twice as long as the fastest one (plus a day), are left out. To choose a specific carrier, pass its id as carrier; if it cannot take the shipment you get a 422 explaining why (e.g. dropoff_not_covered). To choose a specific route from a quote, pass its id as option.

Same body as create, except that the recipient's phone is optional, so a checkout can show prices as soon as the customer pins the address (it is still required to create the shipment). Express delivery

Send "service": "express" (in quotes and when creating) for orders that cannot wait: meals, fresh produce, urgent items. One courier takes the parcel straight from the pickup to the recipient, with no hub in between, and the shipment is promised within the carrier's express time (often an hour).

  • Only carriers that offer express take part, within their express distance, weight limit and opening hours, and only while at least one of their couriers is on shift. Relay chains are never offered for express.
  • Both addresses need a map pin (location), or a saved pickup place with one. Otherwise: 400 with rule express_needs_pin.
  • The price comes from the carrier's express card (a price per trip plus a price per km); cash-on-delivery fees and surcharges are as usual.
  • Quote options and shipments carry "service": "express", etaHours is the express time (e.g. 0.75), and promisedBy is the time it is due. GET /carriers lists each carrier's express offer (minutes, maxKm, maxWeightKg, hours) or null.
  • When nobody can take it right now: 422 no_express_available; for a carrier you named: express_not_offered, express_too_far, express_too_heavy, express_closed, express_no_courier. Fall back to standard delivery.

Live updates

GET /shipments/{id}/stream

A Server-Sent Events stream. The first event is tracking (the full tracking object), then:

  • courier.position — the courier moved (same shape as courier above);
  • tracking.event — the status changed: { status, statusTextAr, statusTextEn, event }.

For browsers, use the public stream (/api/public/track/{token}/stream), which needs no key.

Webhooks

Set a URL under Developers → Webhooks. Turf sends a POST for every change to your shipments:

POST /your/webhook
Turf-Event: shipment.updated
Turf-Delivery: 6ac2…
Turf-Signature: t=1791123456,v1=5f2b…

{
  "id": "6ac2…",
  "type": "shipment.updated",          // or "shipment.created"
  "createdAt": "2026-10-04T12:41:07.000Z",
  "data": {
    "shipment": { … same as GET /shipments/{id} … },
    "event": { "code": "delivered", "at": "…", "textEn": "Delivered", "textAr": "تم التسليم" }
  }
}

Besides status changes, event.code can be details_updated, rescheduled, handed_over (the next company took the parcel), damage_reported, customer_return_requested, recipient_rated and recipient_problem (after delivery the recipient rated it 1–5 or reported a problem from the tracking page; the shipment then has feedback).

Answer with any 2xx within 10 seconds. Failed deliveries are retried after 1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 2 h, 6 h, 12 h and 24 h. Use Turf-Delivery to ignore duplicates.

Verify the signature

Compute HMAC-SHA256 of "{t}.{raw request body}" with your signing secret and compare it with v1. Reject requests older than 5 minutes.

// Node.js / Express
import crypto from 'node:crypto';
app.post('/turf/webhook', express.raw({ type: 'application/json' }), (req, res) => {
  const header = req.get('Turf-Signature') || '';
  const { t, v1 } = Object.fromEntries(header.split(',').map(p => p.split('=')));
  const expected = crypto.createHmac('sha256', process.env.TURF_WEBHOOK_SECRET)
    .update(`${t}.${req.body}`).digest('hex');
  const fresh = Math.abs(Date.now() / 1000 - Number(t)) < 300;
  if (!fresh || !v1 || !crypto.timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(v1), Buffer.from(expected))) {
    return res.sendStatus(400);
  }
  const event = JSON.parse(req.body);
  // update your order with event.data.shipment.status …
  res.sendStatus(200);
});
// PHP
$body = file_get_contents('php://input');
parse_str(str_replace(',', '&', $_SERVER['HTTP_TURF_SIGNATURE'] ?? ''), $sig);
$expected = hash_hmac('sha256', $sig['t'] . '.' . $body, getenv('TURF_WEBHOOK_SECRET'));
if (abs(time() - (int)$sig['t']) > 300 || !hash_equals($expected, $sig['v1'] ?? '')) {
  http_response_code(400); exit;
}
$event = json_decode($body, true);

Tracking widget

Show a live tracking map with status history on your order page — no key needed, two lines:

<div data-turf-tracking="TRACKING_TOKEN" data-lang="ar"></div>
<script src="https://YOUR-TURF-HOST/embed.js" async></script>

Optional data-height="560". If you add elements later (single-page apps), call TurfTracking.refresh(). You can also link to trackingUrl or put embedUrl in your own <iframe>.

Public tracking API

For your own tracking UI in the browser or a mobile app. Authorized by the shipment's secret trackingToken; CORS is open.

GET /api/public/track/{token}?lang=ar

GET /api/public/lookup?n=TRF…,TRF… — by tracking number, no token

GET /api/public/track/{token}/stream (Server-Sent Events, as above)

POST /api/public/track/{token}/location { "lat": 33.52, "lng": 36.27 }

The public view hides contact details and the exact address. The location endpoint lets the recipient share their exact location before delivery (the tracking page has a button for this).

/lookup is the search on Turf's home page and /track: up to 10 tracking numbers (comma or new line between them; case, spaces and dashes don't matter), 20 requests per minute per IP. It answers { "results": [...] } in the order asked: { "query", "found": false }, or found: true with the status, service, promisedBy, the carrier, the store's name, the origin and destination city, and the milestones. It never shows the delivery code, the courier, the recipient's details or cash amounts: those need the token.

const es = new EventSource(`https://YOUR-TURF-HOST/api/public/track/${token}/stream`);
es.addEventListener('courier.position', e => {

});
es.addEventListener('tracking.event', e => showStatus(JSON.parse(e.data).statusTextAr));

Change a shipment

PATCH /shipments/{id}

{ "dropoff": { "phone": "0944999888", "street": "Abu Rummaneh St", "area": null }, "notes": "Call before arriving" }

Fix the recipient's name, phone, city, area, street, details or location (send null to clear an optional field), and notes. Possible until the parcel is out for delivery. The governorate cannot change (the price depends on it): cancel and create a new shipment instead. The change appears in the events as details_updated.

POST /shipments/{id}/reschedule { "date": "2026-10-09" }

The day the recipient can receive the parcel, from today up to 14 days ahead (Syria time). The carrier keeps the parcel until that day. { "date": null } means "as soon as possible" again. Recipients can choose the day themselves on the tracking page, and couriers can record it after a failed attempt. The shipment shows it as scheduledFor.

Cancel

POST /shipments/{id}/cancel { "reason": "Customer changed their mind" }

Only before pickup (awaiting_pickup). After pickup, contact the carrier to return the parcel.

Many shipments at once

POST /shipments/bulk

Up to 500 shipments per request, 10 requests per minute. Each row is either a normal shipment object (as in create) or a flat row with these columns — the same as the CSV upload in the dashboard:

reference, recipient_name, recipient_phone, governorate, city, area, street, details, lat, lng, weight_kg, pieces, description, value, cod_amount, fragile, notes, carrier, strategy, length_cm, width_cm, height_cm, pickup, service

lat and lng pin the recipient; with a pin, governorate and city may be empty. governorate accepts the code, the English or the Arabic name (DI, Damascus, دمشق). pickup is the id or the name of a saved pickup place (empty: your default place). Rows are created one by one: a bad row doesn't stop the others.

{
  "created": 2,
  "failed": 1,
  "results": [
    { "row": 1, "ok": true, "shipment": { … } },
    { "row": 2, "ok": true, "shipment": { … } },
    { "row": 3, "ok": false, "error": { "code": "invalid_request", "message": "dropoff.phone must be a valid phone number", "field": "dropoff.phone", "rule": "phone" } }
  ]
}

A failed row's error has the same shape as any other error: a validation problem may also carry rule and params, so you can show your own message in your customer's language.

With a reference per row, sending the same file again is safe: existing shipments are returned, not duplicated.

Returns & claims

POST /shipments/{id}/return-request { "reason": "Wrong size" }

Your customer sends a delivered parcel back (up to 30 days after delivery). Turf creates a linked return shipment from the customer to your pickup address, with the same company when it can take it. The new shipment has "kind": "customer_return" and returnOf; the original gets returnShipmentId.

POST /shipments/{id}/claims

{ "type": "damaged", "amount": 150000, "description": "The screen arrived cracked" }

type: lost (picked up and not delivered), damaged (after delivery, or after the company reported damage) or late (delivered after promisedBy). Up to 60 days after the shipment, one open claim per parcel. The shipping company accepts (possibly for a different amount) or rejects it with a reason; accepted compensation appears in your statement with that company.

GET /claims — your claims and their status (open, accepted, rejected, withdrawn).

Test mode

Create a test key under Developers → API keys (it starts with turf_test_). With it every endpoint works the same, but shipments are practice shipments: no shipping company sees them, they have no fees, and they never appear in your real lists or reports. Practice quotes come from a fixed test carrier.

POST /shipments/{id}/simulate { "to": "delivered" }

Moves a practice shipment forward, step by step, firing the same webhooks as a real one. to: picked_up, at_hub, in_transit, out_for_delivery, delivered, delivery_failed, returned. Use it to test your integration end to end before going live.

Errors & limits

HTTP/1.1 400 Bad Request
{ "error": { "code": "invalid_request", "message": "dropoff.phone must be a valid phone number", "field": "dropoff.phone", "rule": "phone" } }

Validation errors also say which rule failed (required, number, between, max_length, phone, one_of, …) with its params (e.g. { "min": 0, "max": 100 }), so you can show your own message in any language. Other errors have a specific code (and sometimes params); message is always a readable English sentence.

Limits: 600 requests per minute per key; public tracking 120 per minute per IP. Every response carries RateLimit-Limit, RateLimit-Remaining and RateLimit-Reset.

ملخص بالعربية

يتيح لك الـ API إنشاء الشحنات من خادم متجرك، فيُسندها Turf فوراً إلى شركة شحن: التي تختارها (carrier)، أو الأفضل تلقائياً ("auto") حسب استراتيجية strategy: الأفضل إجمالاً، الأرخص، الأسرع، الأقرب، أو الأكثر موثوقية.

  • أرسل المفتاح السري في ترويسة Authorization: Bearer turf_sk_… ومن خادمك فقط.
  • استخدم reference برقم طلبك كي لا تتكرر الشحنة عند إعادة المحاولة.
  • يعيد الرد رابط تتبع (trackingUrl) لإرساله للزبون، ورابط ملصق للطباعة (labelUrl)، ورمز تسليم (deliveryCode) يعطيه المستلم للمندوب.
  • واجهة التتبع تعيد الحالة نصاً بالعربية والإنجليزية، وسجل الأحداث، وموقع المندوب المباشر أثناء حمله للطرد مهما كانت وسيلته.
  • اضبط Webhook لتصلك كل تغييرات الحالة موقّعة بـ HMAC-SHA256.
  • لعرض خريطة تتبع مباشرة في متجرك أضف سطرين: <div data-turf-tracking="…"> و embed.js.
  • مفاتيح turf_test_ للتجربة: شحنات تجريبية لا تصل لأي شركة، ويمكنك تحريكها بـ /simulate لاختبار الـ Webhooks.
  • حتى 500 شحنة في طلب واحد عبر /shipments/bulk، وتعديل معلومات المستلم بـ PATCH، وتحديد يوم التسليم بـ /reschedule.
  • إرجاع الزبون للطرد بـ /return-request، والمطالبات (فقدان، تلف، تأخير) بـ /claims.
  • يعرض POST /quotes كل المسارات المعقولة: شركات منفردة وسلاسل من حتى ثلاث شركات تتسلّم الطرد بالتتابع (route)، بشحنة ورقم تتبع وسعر واحد. مرّر id المسار في الحقل option عند الإنشاء لاختياره.
  • للوجبات والمواد الطازجة أرسل "service": "express": توصيل خلال الساعة تقريباً من مكان الاستلام إلى المستلم مباشرة، من الشركات التي تقدّمه ولديها مندوب في وردية. يحتاج العنوانان إلى موقع على الخريطة.
  • أخطاء التحقق تتضمن rule وparams لعرض رسالة بلغتك.
